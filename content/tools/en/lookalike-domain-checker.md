## What this tool checks

Scammers copy the website name of a company you trust and change it just enough that you do not notice. An email from `sales@alibabba.com` or a payment page on `paypa1-secure.com` looks right at a quick glance.

Type the website or email address you got. If you know the company's real website, add it in the second box, and the tool compares the two. If you leave it empty, the tool compares the name with famous brands like Amazon, PayPal, Alibaba, DHL and the big B2B marketplaces.

## The tricks it finds

| Trick | Fake | Real |
|---|---|---|
| One letter changed, added, left out or swapped | `alibabba.com`, `amazn.com`, `goolge.com` | `alibaba.com`, `amazon.com`, `google.com` |
| A number for a letter | `paypa1.com`, `amaz0n.com` | `paypal.com`, `amazon.com` |
| Letters that look alike together | `rnicrosoft.com` (rn looks like m) | `microsoft.com` |
| Letters from another alphabet | `аpple.com` (a Cyrillic "а") | `apple.com` |
| An extra word or dash | `paypal-secure.com`, `pay-pal.com` | `paypal.com` |
| Another ending | `paypal.co` | `paypal.com` |
| The real name in front of another website | `paypal.com.login-help.xyz` | `paypal.com` |

The last one fools many people: the address starts with the real name, but the website is really `login-help.xyz`. What counts is the part just before the ending, not the start.

## How to read the result

| Result | What it means |
|---|---|
| Lookalike | A copy with a trick. Do not sign in, pay or download anything from it. |
| Close | Close to the real name, but not a clear copy. It may be the company's website for another country. Ask the company. |
| Not a copy | A different name. It is not the real company's website either. |
| Real website | The same website as the real one, or a part of it like `shop.paypal.com`. |

> **Tip:** A changed bank account that comes in an email from a lookalike address is one of the most common B2B payment scams. Before you pay new bank details, call the supplier on a phone number you found yourself, not one in the email.

## Questions

### Is this tool free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no limit. The check runs on our server in a moment and does not look anything up on the internet.

### Does "Not a copy" mean the website is safe?

No. It only means the name does not copy the company you gave. Check how old the website is with the [Domain Age Checker](/tools/domain-age-checker) and look for the company in its official register.

### Do you save the addresses I check?

No. The address is checked and the answer is shown to you. Nothing is kept.

### Can I check a supplier automatically?

Yes. The free Safely Chrome extension checks lookalike names, and 14 other signals, every time you open a supplier page on Alibaba and other B2B marketplaces.
