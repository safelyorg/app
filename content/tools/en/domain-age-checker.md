## What this tool checks

Every website address (domain) is registered with an official registry. That record shows **when the domain was first registered**, **when it expires** and **which company it was registered through** (the registrar).

This tool reads that public record and tells you how old the website is, in plain words. It works with links too: paste `https://www.supplier.com/about` and it checks `supplier.com`.

## How to read the result

| Result | Age | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Very new | Under 6 months | A red flag if the company says it has been in business for years. Check more before you pay. |
| Fairly new | 6 months to 2 years | Not a problem by itself. Compare it with the history the company claims. |
| Established | Over 2 years | A good sign, but not proof. Old domains can be bought or taken over. |

If the result also says **"Expires in less than 3 months"**, the domain may have been registered for one year only. Scam websites are often set up this way, because they do not plan to stay long.

## Why domain age matters when you buy from a supplier

A fake supplier can copy photos, make a nice website and answer emails quickly. What they cannot fake is **time**. A company that claims "20 years of experience" but has a website registered a few weeks ago is telling you two different stories.

> **Tip:** Domain age is one check, not a verdict. A new website can belong to a real company that just moved online. Use it together with the company registry, the payment terms and a phone call on a number you found yourself.

## Questions

### Is this tool free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no limit for normal use. To stop abuse, each visitor can run 30 checks an hour.

### Why does it say the registration date is not public?

Some country registries do not share the creation date. In that case, check the company in its official business registry instead.

### Do you save the websites I check?

No. We look up the public record and show it to you. The answer is kept for a few hours so the next check of the same website is faster, but it is never linked to you.

### Can I check a supplier automatically?

Yes. The free Safely Chrome extension checks domain age, and 14 other signals, every time you open a supplier page on Alibaba and other B2B marketplaces.
