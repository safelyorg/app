Safely vs seller reviews

Stars and reviews are useful. They cannot help with a seller who has no history yet.

The short answer

Reviews and star ratings help, but they only exist after other people have bought. A new seller has none, and a scam supplier can be new. Safely does not need a review history. It checks the listing, the seller and the company.

What reviews tell you

Star ratings and reviews on a marketplace are useful. A long history of good reviews is a good sign. The limit is that reviews are built from past sales.

A brand-new seller account has no reviews yet. That tells you nothing either way, and it is the situation a new scam listing is in. Reviews also describe a past order, not whether this supplier is who they say they are.

What Safely checks instead

Safely does not depend on a review history existing. It reads the supplier page and runs 15 signals across the listing, the seller and the company. Examples are:

  • Listing: price compared with similar listings, urgency language, an advance payment request, image authenticity, and how complete the listing is.
  • Seller: account age, what the platform's verification badge really covers, contact details, the seller's website, and community fraud reports.
  • Company: whether the name, address and registration number match, how complete the company profile is, and the domain age.

Use both

Reviews are still worth reading when they exist. Use Safely to check the parts a review cannot show, especially for a supplier you have never bought from.

Safely gives a risk score from 0 to 100 as guidance. It is not a guarantee, and it does not replace calling the supplier on a phone number you already had.
