What Safely catches

The warning signs you'd miss.

Each supplier is checked on 15 signals. These are the ones that matter most before you pay.

Risky payment terms

Full payment before shipping, or untraceable methods like Western Union.

Brand-new companies

Suppliers founded this year, with no track record to rely on.

Unverified profiles

No platform verification, and contact details that don't add up.

Vague listings

Copy-paste product pages with no real price, order or delivery details.

Licensed products

Products that need a licence, sold by someone who isn't the maker.

Reported by other buyers

Suppliers that other Safely users have already reported as scams.

Questions

Before you install.

Is it really free?+

Yes. Every account gets 10 supplier scans a month with no credit card. They come back every month on the day you signed up.

Which sites does it work on?+

Alibaba, TradeWheel, ExportHub, B2Brazil, ThomasNet, B2BMap and Kompass. Open a product or company page and the check starts by itself.

Does it work on my phone?+

Not yet. Safely is a Chrome extension, so it runs in Chrome on a laptop or desktop computer.

What data does Safely collect?+

Only the supplier and listing details on the pages you open on supported marketplaces. We never track your other browsing and never sell your data.

Is the score a guarantee?+

No. It's a fast, clear check that shows you the warning signs. The final decision is always yours.
