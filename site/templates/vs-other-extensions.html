Safely vs other extensions

A website rating tells you about the marketplace. Buyers need an answer about the supplier.

The short answer

Many scam-check extensions rate a whole website. On a marketplace, the website is trusted by everyone, and the risk is one supplier. Safely is built for that case. It reads the individual supplier page on 7 B2B marketplaces.

Why a website rating is not enough

A website-trust extension usually looks at the domain you are on. That works for a small online shop with its own site. On a marketplace like Alibaba or ThomasNet, the domain is well known, so a domain rating looks fine for every supplier on it, honest or not.

The question a buyer needs answered is about one supplier: is this company real, and does the listing match it?

What Safely checks

Safely reads the individual supplier page and runs 15 signals on the listing, the seller and the company. It adds a check of the supplier's presence on social networks and review sites, and a check for regulated products. It supports Alibaba, TradeWheel, ExportHub, B2Brazil, ThomasNet, B2BMap and Kompass. It only runs on individual listing pages, not on a marketplace's home page or search results.

What to look for in any scam-check extension

  • Does it work on the pages where you actually buy?
  • Does it look at the supplier, or only at the domain?
  • Does it show the evidence behind its answer, or only a label?
  • Does it explain what data it collects and what it does with it?
  • Is there a free plan you can try on a real supplier first?
