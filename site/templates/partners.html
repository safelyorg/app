FAQ

Partner questions.

Who pays me, and how?

Our payment partner, Creem, tracks your referrals and pays you directly by bank transfer or USDC. Before your first payout, Creem asks for a quick ID check. Payout fees: bank transfer $7 or 1% (whichever is higher), USDC 2%.

When does a sale count as mine?

When someone clicks your link and subscribes within 180 days, using the same browser. The sale appears in your dashboard automatically.

When can I withdraw?

Each commission is held for 7–12 days in case of a refund. Once your available balance reaches $50, click Withdraw and it is paid on the next 1st or 15th of the month.

Do I need to be a Safely customer?

No. Anyone can join — sourcing agents, consultants, trade communities, newsletter writers. You only need an email address to sign in.

Are there any rules?

Be honest about what Safely does, don't send spam, and don't refer yourself. Questions? Email help@safely.sh.
