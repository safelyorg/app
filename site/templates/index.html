01 Is there a free plan?

Yes — every account gets 50 free scans every month, with no credit card needed. They come back every month on the day you signed up. If you need more, upgrade to Team (750 scans a month) or Enterprise (unlimited) at any time.

02 Which B2B platforms does Safely support?

Safely is fully live on Alibaba, TradeWheel, ExportHub, B2Brazil, ThomasNet, B2BMap, and Kompass — with more B2B marketplaces added regularly. See the next question for exactly which pages currently trigger an analysis.

03 How does the AI analyze listings?

When you open a supplier page, Safely reads the listing details, company profile, registration information, and contact data from the page. This is combined with a real-time check of the supplier's presence across the web, then sent to Claude AI to analyze 15 distinct signals and return a risk score from 0–100 with a detailed breakdown of every finding.

04 Who sees my fraud reports?

Fraud reports are shared across the Safely community — they are visible to any user who views the same seller. Your identity is never attached to a report. Reports only show the reason and timestamp.

05 Does Safely slow my browser down?

No. The extension only activates on supported marketplace pages and runs analysis in the background without blocking page load. Analysis typically completes in about 10 seconds and has no noticeable impact on browser performance.

06 Is my browsing data private?

We only collect listing and seller data from pages you actively visit on supported marketplaces. We never track your browsing history, never sell your data to third parties, and all analysis data is stored securely. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

07 Which specific pages does Safely actually scan?

Right now, Safely actively reads supplier and listing pages on Alibaba, TradeWheel, ExportHub, B2Brazil, ThomasNet, B2BMap, and Kompass. Safely only activates on individual listing pages — simply browsing a platform's homepage or search results won't trigger an analysis. We're continuously adding support for more B2B platforms.

Safely vs Norton

Norton and similar antivirus tools protect your computer — they catch malware, viruses, and malicious files trying to attack your device.

Safely protects you from a different kind of attack entirely: a real person on the other end of a listing, manipulating your judgement into sending money for something that doesn't exist. No file is downloaded, no malware is involved — which is exactly why antivirus software has nothing to detect. Safely and antivirus solve two different problems, and most people benefit from both.

Got it

Safely vs manual research

Most buyers today check a listing by reading it carefully, searching the seller's name, and going with their gut. That works reasonably well for obvious scams, but takes several minutes per listing and still misses those signals — recycled images, slightly-off pricing, or urgency language designed specifically to bypass exactly that kind of gut check.

Safely runs the same checks a careful buyer would want to do, automatically, in about 10 seconds, every time you open a listing.

Got it

Safely vs basic seller reviews

Star ratings and reviews built into marketplaces are useful, but a brand-new seller account naturally has none yet — which tells you nothing either way, and is exactly the situation most scam listings are posted from.

Safely doesn't rely on a review history existing at all. It analyzes the listing itself — the language used, the images, the price, the seller's account age — so it can flag risk even on a seller's very first post.

Got it

Safely vs generic browser extensions

Ad blockers, price trackers, and coupon extensions are built to change or add something to a page — they were never designed to evaluate whether the person selling to you is trustworthy.

Safely is purpose-built around one question: is this specific listing and seller safe to send money to. That's the only thing it does, which is why it can go deep on it — 15 distinct fraud signals, seller history, and a community fraud report network, all in one focused tool.
